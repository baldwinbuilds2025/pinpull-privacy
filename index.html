Pin-Pull Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026
App: Pin-Pull (Android)
Developer: Baldwin Builds

Overview

Pin-Pull is a single-player physics puzzle game. The current version works offline, does not require an account, and does not include advertisements, in-app purchases, analytics, or tracking SDKs.

Information we collect

Baldwin Builds does not collect personal information or device information through Pin-Pull. The game does not request your name, email address, location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone.

Your taps, puzzle outcomes, and level progression are processed on your device. In this version, progress is kept only while the app is running; it is not uploaded or stored on a developer-operated server.

How information is used and shared

We do not sell, rent, or share personal data from Pin-Pull with advertisers, data brokers, or other third parties. The game does not send gameplay data to us.

If you download or install Pin-Pull through Google Play, Google may process information about your Play Store activity, installations, or diagnostics under Google's own privacy policy. That processing is separate from data collected by Pin-Pull.

Data security, retention, and deletion

Because this version does not collect personal data or maintain user accounts or developer-operated player records, Baldwin Builds has no personal data from the game to retain or delete. Gameplay state exists in temporary app memory and is cleared when the app session ends. You can remove the app from your device at any time.

Children's privacy

Pin-Pull does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the game's features or data practices change. Updates will be published in the app and on this webpage, with a revised effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions or requests, use the developer contact information shown on the Pin-Pull Google Play store listing.
